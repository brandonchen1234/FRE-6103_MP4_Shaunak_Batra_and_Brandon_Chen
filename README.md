Dear class:

I thought you might like to do a structuring exercise.  Using the Farmer Brown case from the futures lecture, I would like you to design a proper hedging strategy for the corn farmer.  There will be some extensions of the Farmer Brown situation to consider.  For all these extensions, please make suitable assumptions about the parameters; you will be graded on the logic of your recommended strategy and analysis, which should not exceed one page (excluding graphs).

There will be volumetric production risk, where the number of bushels is given by a probability distribution.  
There will be costs.
There will be drought risk
There will be basis risk.
There will be margining risk, but you should decide whether Farmer Brown should use futures or OTC futures, which offer credit lines for margin calls at a fee.
You may use both options and futures.
You will probably be doing some simulations.
Assume that the Farmer's objective is to minimize the variance of sales proceeds from the harvest.  Please make sure to answer the following questions:

a.  What assumptions did you make?

b.  How did you perform the analysis?

c.  What is your recommended strategy?  How did it differ from what you expected?

d.  How does your recommended strategy differ from the futures hedging strategy we discussed?

Enjoy!

Prof Shimko
